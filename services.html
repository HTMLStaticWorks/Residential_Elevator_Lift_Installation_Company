Workflow

Our 6-Step Project Cycle

A transparent engineering schedule coordinates tasks from site dimensions to final safety certification handouts.

Step 01

Initial Dimensions

Inspecting concrete slab depths, vertical shaft alignments, and structural load limits.

Step 02

Finishes Planning

Selecting interior cabin textures, automatic sliding doors, and control system displays.

Step 03

Drawing Drafting

Drafting official structural clearance layouts and safety loads calculations.

Step 04

Materials Prep

Manufacturing the steel guide columns, pump units, and structural frames.

Step 05

Assembly & Setup

On-site assembly of guide rails, cabin frames, motor fixtures, and safety brakes.

Step 06

Final Certification

Running structural load tests and handing over safety operating manuals.

Final installation criteria depend strictly on the selected lift models, number of stops, and site conditions.
Existing Homes

Structural Retrofitting Solutions

Adding an elevator to an established residence doesn't require tearing down your house. Our civil engineering teams analyze structural framing to find optimal space integrations with minimal interior impact.

Stairwell Voids

Utilizes empty spaces in wrap-around stairs to mount a steel-and-glass self-supporting structure.

Exterior Facades

Builds a weatherproof panoramic glass enclosure on the outer wall, opening directly to interior corridors.

Closet Stack Alignments

Replaces stacked closets on adjacent floors with a single vertical shaft opening with minimal architectural adjustments.

Retrofit Feasibility Checklist

Before planning an installation, our technicians verify the following on-site criteria:

Concrete floor slab load tolerance (minimum 250 kg/m²)
Vertical clearance at top level (minimum 2450 mm clear height)
Power feed distribution capacity (Single-phase 220V / Three-phase 380V)
Structural path clearances to transport guide columns into the shaft
