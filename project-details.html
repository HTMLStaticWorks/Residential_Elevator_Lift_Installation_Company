The Coastal Glass Villa

A complete installation review of our circular panoramic glass elevator inside a luxury oceanfront villa staircase.

Elevator System: Apex Panoramic Glass
Shaft Type: Structural glass framework
Floors Served: 3 levels (Basement, Ground, First)
Book Site Assessment Visit

Project Overview & Challenges

The villa layout demanded a vertical transport solution that would not block natural daylight or disrupt the view. The central concrete stairwell left exactly 1300 mm of diameter, requiring custom brackets and safety governors.

Engineering Decisions

Our engineers configured the Apex Panoramic Glass model with a gearless motor. This eliminated the need for a rooftop motor room and allowed installation inside the open spiral stairwell frame.

Installation Characteristics

Concrete Pit: 50 mm shallow
Power Supply: 220V Single-Phase
Cabin Interior: Clear panoramic, chrome details
Load Limit: 400 kg (4-5 passengers)
