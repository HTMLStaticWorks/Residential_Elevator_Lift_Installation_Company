Technical Guidance

Mechanical Drive Selection Matrix

Explore the primary structural drive technologies available for residential installations to find the optimal match for your home's layout.

Gearless Traction

Uses a high-efficiency motor and counterweight. Exceptionally quiet, smooth, and suitable for heights.

  • Smooth travel curve
  • Highly energy-efficient
  • Requires shallow pit

Eco-Hydraulic

Utilizes a hydraulic fluid pump. Features a robust lifting capacity, making it perfect for heavy wood.

  • High weight capacity
  • Quiet pump layout
  • Requires control cabinet

Pneumatic Vacuum

Air pressure-driven system. Self-supporting structure that requires zero shaft construction or concrete pits.

  • 360-degree panoramic view
  • Quick 3-day installation
  • Limited to 250kg load

Screw & Nut Drive

Electrical screw drive. Offers maximum space efficiency with extremely compact dimensions for retrofits.

  • Tiny shaft footprint
  • Zero pit required
  • Slightly slower speed
