Safety Architecture

Integrated Multi-Tier Safety Systems

Our home lifts are engineered with robust backups and protection layers. *Safety systems and configurations vary by elevator model and site specifications.*

Emergency Lowering

Automatic battery backup brings the cabin safely to the next lower floor in a power outage.

Electronic Interlocks

Doors remain locked electro-mechanically unless the cabin is perfectly aligned at the landing zone.

Light Curtains

Full-height infrared light rays detect any obstruction at the doors and instantly reverse opening.

Overload Warning

Pressure and tension sensors alert users and lock travel if the rated weight capacity is exceeded.

Our Process

Seamless Installation Workflow

A highly structured 6-step project cycle ensures structural compliance and exact alignment. *Final installation parameters depend on specific equipment and home conditions.*

01

Assessment

We inspect elevator shaft openings, floor-to-floor structural heights, and electrical layouts during a site visit.

02

Planning

Architects and clients align on travel capacity, cabin finish veneers, door configurations, and speed standards.

03

Design & Spec

Our engineering department issues certified CAD drawing sets containing load schedules and wiring schematics.

04

Procurement

The lift cabin elements, safety frames, and mechanical guide rails are precision manufactured to match drawing specifications.

05

Installation

Certified technicians execute lift assembly, guide-rail tracking, control wiring integration, and stress tests.

06

Handover

We perform final safety inspections, demonstrate manual rescue overrides, and supply the certification package.
