Preventive Service

Precision Engineering Diagnostics

To preserve structural safety margins, residential elevators require systematic inspections. Our service engineers perform deep-level mechanical and electronic inspections.

  • Diagnostic Calibration: Testing controller logs, motor frequency curves, and leveling switch coordinates.
  • Mechanical Calibrations: Tensioning suspension cables, lubricating guide rail rails, and tightening mechanical joints.
  • Safety Checks: Stress testing speed governors, emergency cabin brakes, buffers, and door light barriers.
