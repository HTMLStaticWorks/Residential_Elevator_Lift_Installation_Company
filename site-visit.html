Technical Assessment Workflow

What Our Lift Engineers Evaluate On-Site

Our certified vertical mobility specialists conduct an in-depth, non-invasive physical evaluation to verify that your home can safely and seamlessly integrate a residential lift.

Evaluation Step 01

Shaft & Clearance Diagnostics

Precise laser measurement of travel height, stairwell voids, overhead headroom clearances, and floor-to-floor alignment.

  • Overhead clearance (min. 2400 mm)
  • Pit depth allowance (0 - 200 mm)
  • Stairwell center void geometry
Evaluation Step 02

Structural Load & Slab Integrity

Evaluating concrete slab bearing capacities, load transfer vectors, and anchor wall reinforcements for vibration isolation.

  • Floor slab load ratings
  • Lateral anchor points check
  • Self-supporting shaft framing
Evaluation Step 03

Electrical & Power Infrastructure

Inspection of main distribution panels, breaker availability, power conduits, and backup battery emergency lowering locations.

  • Single-phase 220V / 380V feed
  • Dedicated breaker & surge circuit
  • ARD emergency battery space
Evaluation Step 04

3D Blueprint & Finish Matching

Generating on-site recommendations for landing door swing directions, shaft glazing styles, and customized aesthetic trims.

  • Single / adjacent / opposite doors
  • Panoramic glass vs opaque cabin
  • Interior finish & lighting harmonizing

How to Prepare & What You Receive

We ensure our site assessments are swift, thorough, and zero-hassle. Here is how homeowners can prepare and what deliverables you will receive within 24 hours.

Homeowner Preparation Checklist

Recommended

Having these items or permissions ready helps our engineering team complete the survey in under 45 minutes:

Clear Access to Proposed Installation Zones Ensure stairwells, hallways, and potential shaft footprints are accessible on all intended floors.
Main Electrical Panel Access Allow our technician to inspect electrical sub-boards and breaker panel capacity.
Architectural Floor Plans (If Available) Drawings or blueprints help calculate load distributions faster, though our laser scan will suffice if unavailable.
Key Decision-Makers Present Discuss door orientation preferences, cabin aesthetics, and timeline expectations directly with the engineer.
